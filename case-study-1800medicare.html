The Problem

Australia's health record app fails the people who need it most.

New users face a maze of three separate apps with no guidance. People with chronic conditions can't edit their own information. And when something goes wrong, the only feedback is "No records found" — no explanation, no next step.

This damages trust in a system designed to empower patients.

My Role

End-to-end Designer

This is a self-initiated redesign concept. I conducted all research, defined the scope, created the persona and journey map, designed the wireframes, and produced the hi-fi prototype in Figma, with AI tools supporting ideation and iteration throughout the process.

As a 482 visa holder without Australian Medicare credentials, I couldn't access the app directly. Instead, I based the project on App Store screenshots, official government documentation, public reviews, and first-hand observation of people navigating Australia's healthcare system.

Personal Reflection
"As someone who arrived in Australia without knowing what myGov, myID, or My Health Record were, Mei Lin's journey isn't just a design tool, it's a story I lived."
