About Me

Hi, I'm Marlene.

Designing products that are intuitive for people and practical to build.

I started my career in development and gradually moved toward design after becoming fascinated by the way people interact with digital products.

Today, I combine both perspectives to create experiences that are visually appealing, technically feasible, and centered around real user needs.

I enjoy creating digital experiences that simplify complexity, solve real problems, and make technology feel more intuitive.

My Process

From complexity to clarity.

A collaborative and iterative approach to turning research, design and development into thoughtful digital experiences.

Understand before designing

I take time to learn about the user, the business, and the constraints before making design decisions.

Look for the real friction

I go beyond the surface request to identify what's confusing, what's missing, and where the experience can work better.

Design with intention

I use structure, hierarchy, and systems thinking to turn complexity into interfaces that feel simpler and more intuitive.

Refine as I go

I prototype, test, and iterate early to improve the experience through feedback, not assumptions.

Think through delivery

With a development background, I design with implementation in mind, considering feasibility, responsiveness, and handoff from the start.
